Quantifying Alternative Data: Replicating the REIN Framework for Commercial Credit Risk



Executive Summary:
Traditional credit risk models rely on historical financial statements, which means they often flag trouble after a borrower is already in distress. Inspired by the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta’s Regional Economic Information Network (REIN) framework, which uses ground-level business intelligence to anticipate economic shifts, this project builds an automated pipeline that turns unstructured market news into real-time risk indicators.

By scraping local commercial real estate (CRE) market commentary and processing it through a Generative AI feature engineering pipeline, unstructured text is converted into quantitative distress scores. When added to standard balance-sheet metrics like Loan-to-Value and Debt Service Coverage ratios, these AI-derived qualitative signals improved a Logistic Regression and Random Forest model’s ability to predict loan default probability, giving risk managers a true leading indicator before defaults occur.
